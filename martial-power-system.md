# Martial Power System

**Status:** Early core concept.

The martial power scale is currently influenced by two sources of inspiration. The setting is fictional; these inspirations are being adapted into its own internal rules.

## The Level 1–9 framework

The world uses a broad martial power scale from **Level 1 to Level 9**.

### Levels 1–7: the human range

The concept of seven levels is inspired by the idea of **Kundalini and the seven chakra centres**.

In this setting, **Level 7 is the natural human ceiling**.

A human may become extraordinarily skilled, disciplined and powerful within the normal limits of the world, but simply training harder does not automatically allow them to pass Level 7.

This gives Level 7 a special status: it represents the highest level history normally recognises as achievable by a human being.

### Level 8 and Level 9: beyond the normal human ceiling

Levels 8 and 9 are inspired by ideas discussed by Kim Goguen and are being adapted as fictional world-building concepts.

They represent states or sources of power **beyond the ordinary human range**.

A character reaching these levels must have an explanation for why they have crossed the normal ceiling. Something else happened.

The exact mechanisms have not yet been defined.

Possible future categories include:

- A rare external source of power.
- An altered state of the body or spirit.
- A unique substance or artefact.
- A transformation with a significant cost.

These are possibilities, not fixed rules.

## Design principle: crossing the ceiling needs a reason

The purpose of Levels 8 and 9 is not to create an endless escalation ladder.

The rule is:

> **Level 7 is the human ceiling. Level 8 or 9 requires an exceptional cause, and exceptional power must have consequences or limitations.**

This allows extraordinary characters to exist without making them the new normal.

## The Right Hand and the royal elixir

The story of **The Two Hands** is the first major test of this rule.

The Right Hand's natural ceiling is around Level 5–6. He cannot train his way into permanent Level 8 status.

A stolen royal elixir temporarily gives him access to power comparable to Level 8. The power is limited and eventually exhausted.

This means his extraordinary combat displays do not invalidate the Level 7 human ceiling.

## Current reference points

These are broad narrative classifications rather than precise mathematical measurements:

- **Level 5:** Warlord level.
- **Level 6:** Elite.
- **Level 7:** The highest historically recognised human level; top S-class territory.
- **Level 8:** Beyond the normal human ceiling; requires an exceptional cause.
- **Level 9:** Beyond Level 8 and not yet defined.

## Open questions

- What exactly distinguishes a Level 7 from a Level 6 in combat?
- Is Level 7 a single peak, or can two Level 7 fighters have radically different strengths?
- What kinds of events can allow someone to cross into Level 8?
- What is Level 9, and should it be reachable at all?
- How should the seven chakra-inspired stages be adapted into the world's own mythology without simply copying real-world beliefs?
