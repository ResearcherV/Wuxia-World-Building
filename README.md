# Wuxia World-Building

A shared wuxia world designed to support many stories, characters, creators, and formats — including manga, games, and other works.

The world should feel like a living society rather than a collection of martial artists waiting for the next duel. Political authority, commerce, information, territory, reputation, alliances, and martial ability all shape events.

## The guiding idea

**The world is bigger than any one protagonist.**

Characters may rise, fall, disappear, die, become legends, or return in a different role. The setting continues beyond them. Different creators should be able to tell very different stories in the same world while respecting its established foundations.

## Project principles

- Seven major kingdoms are the current core political concept.
- Power is distributed; no single ruler or organisation automatically controls everything.
- Martial artists and organisations need economic foundations to survive.
- Conflict can be political, commercial, territorial, social, or personal — it does not always end in death.
- Martial strength has limits. Avoid endless power escalation.
- Winning a fight does not necessarily mean winning the larger conflict.
- The world should support many protagonists and independent storylines.

## How to read this archive

This project distinguishes between:

- **Established foundations** — ideas stated clearly by the world creator.
- **Working proposals** — ideas suggested during development that may be revised.
- **Open questions** — areas that have not yet been decided.

A proposal is not automatically fixed canon. The creator may accept, change, combine, or reject it.

## Current development areas

1. World principles and shared-world rules
2. The Seven Kingdoms and their political structures
3. Martial arts, ability limits, and combat
4. Clans, sects, gangs, guilds, and other organisations
5. Trade, money, resources, and livelihoods
6. Characters, stories, and continuity
